Our story

From a small workshop to an industrial force.

“The dream grows when honest effort, patience, and teamwork walk together from small beginnings to great achievements.”

From small beginnings to big dreams.

SJS Hydraulics was founded in 2007 by a vision of practical engineering and dependable execution. What began in a modest setup grew into a trusted manufacturing identity rooted in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Behind every milestone is the quiet strength of dedicated people— our father and his friends who believed that honest hard work, discipline, and unity can shape not only machines but lasting legacy.

Today, SJS is recognized for durable machines, process-focused design, and strong client relationships built on transparency, performance, and long-term support.
