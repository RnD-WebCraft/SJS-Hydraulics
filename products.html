Detailed Machinery Profiles

Iron Scrap Bundling Machine: A precision-engineered solution specifically optimized to streamline high-volume scrap management and Industrial recycling workflows

  • Double Press Upward Ejection Bundling Machine: This machine utilizes a dual compression system to pack scrap tightly from two sides. The finished bundle is ejected vertically(upward), making it an ideal solution for facilities with limited floor space or those using overhead cranes for material handling.

  • Double Press Front Ejection Bundling Machine: Similar to the upward ejection model this machine employs double-press technology for high-density bundling. However, the bundles are pushed out through the front of the machine, allowing for seamless integration with conveyor belts or horizontal discharge systems.

  • Triple Press Upward Ejection Bundling Machine: Designed for high-capacity industrial scrap, this machine applies pressure from there distinct direction to create the most compact bundles possible. The upward ejection mechanism facilitates easy retrieval in heavy-duty recycling environments.

Iron Scrap Cutting Machine: Features Heavy-duty, high-performance blades and advanced systems designed for consistent, high-precision cutting of dense iron scrap

  • Mobile type Iron Scrap Cutting Machine: Delivers uncompromising industrial grade cutting power with added advantage of on-site portability for flexible job site management

  • Double Gear Iron Scrap Cutting Machine: Enhanced with a specialized dual-gear system to provide increased torque and mechanical efficiency during the toughest industrial cutting tasks.

Iron Scrap Bailing Press: Our Iron Scrap Bailing press is masterfully designed for maximum high-pressure compression. It is specifically engineered to handle large volumes of Iron Scrap, significantly improving efficiency in heavy-duty recycling operation, storage and transportation.

Iron Sheet Rolling machine: Beyond scrap processing, SJS Hydraulics provides precision sheet rolling machines designed for industrial fabrication. These machines are built with high grade materials to ensure surgical mechanical integrity and high tolerance levels, allowing for the precise curving anf shaping of metal sheets to eet mission-critical engineering specifications.

Hydraulic Power Pack: Hydraulic Power pack serves as the high-performance heart of industrial machinery, engineered to provide consistent and reliable fluid power in the most demanding environments. These units are meticulously designed to convert mechanical energy into hydraulic pressure, ensuring your heavy-duty operations run with surgical precision.
